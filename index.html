The Living Compass

Most of us spend our lives responding to what's in front of us without taking the time to deeply examine who we are, what we believe, and what we really want.

The Living Compass changes that. Through guided reflection and meaningful questions, it helps you examine your life, discover what matters most, and develop a clear direction for the future you choose.

Along the way, you're building something valuable: a personal AI brain grounded in your own examined life. Not a collection of memories and conversations. An evolving understanding of your values, experiences, strengths, priorities, and aspirations. A brain that doesn't just remember where you've been. It helps you stay oriented toward where you've chosen to go.

The Living Compass isn't about letting artificial intelligence decide where your life should go. It's about discovering your own direction, and learning to use AI to help you get there.
