A Little About Me — And Why I'm Doing This

I'm Paul Schooler, a Navy veteran, lifelong entrepreneur, father of five, and grandfather of six.

I've spent more than 35 years in direct sales, recruiting, and team building. Throughout that time, my greatest satisfaction has always come from helping ordinary people discover what they're capable of and develop the confidence to build something of their own.

But when I turned 60, I found myself asking some of the same questions many people are asking today.

The world is changing so rapidly. Where do I fit in? What's next for me? And how do I prepare for a future that looks completely different from the past?

I realized artificial intelligence was going to change nearly everything about how we work, build businesses, and live our lives.

So I decided to learn it.

Over the past year, I've invested approximately 1,500 hours studying AI, experimenting with new tools, and learning how this technology can help ordinary people accomplish things that once required entire teams.

Over the last six months, I've also been learning from the Rockstar AI training community while developing something I call The Living Compass.

Compass grew out of a simple idea:

What if I could take everything I've learned through hundreds and hundreds of hours of study and turn it into a simple system that could save other people that time?

A system to help people understand AI, discover what they really want out of life, develop their own personal AI brain, and use these new tools to create more freedom and opportunity.

Originally, I intended to offer Compass as a paid education and personal development program. It was going to be my next business and my way of earning a living in this new AI economy.

Then something changed.

Powur introduced its new AI Node opportunity, bringing together two of the most important technologies shaping our future: energy and artificial intelligence.

Suddenly, I saw a way to combine everything I'd spent my career doing with everything I'd been learning.

Powur could become the business vehicle. Compass could become the system that helps people grow into that opportunity.

So I changed my plan.

Instead of charging for Compass, I've decided to make it available at no additional cost to people who choose to build a Powur business with me.

I'll share what I've learned, the tools I've developed, and the training systems I'm continuing to build.

Not because I have all the answers. I'm still learning every day.

But because I believe people shouldn't have to spend thousands of hours figuring out what I've already spent the last year learning.

I want to help them shorten that learning curve, develop confidence with AI, and build a business with the support of a team.

I've always believed that the greatest opportunity isn't simply making money.

It's becoming the kind of person who can create value, lead others, and build a better future.

That's what I'm building.

And if that sounds like the kind of journey you'd like to be part of, I'd love to explore it with you.

Automate the repeatable. Elevate the human.

Paul Schooler

Building People. Developing Leaders. Creating Opportunity.
